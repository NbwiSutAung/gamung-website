Privacy Policy

Effective: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information Gămung collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. "Gămung", "we" and "us" mean the team that runs the Gămung app. If you have a question, write to gamung.app@gmail.com or use Help & Support in the app.

The short version

  • We collect what you give us (your profile, posts, listings, messages) and a little technical information needed to run the app.
  • We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't use your phone's GPS. Your location is the township you choose.
  • Your birth date, gender and interests are private. Other people never see them.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings, and most of your information is deleted with it right away.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address, or your Google or Apple sign-in, and a password if you use one.
  • Profile: your name, photo, bio, any links you add (website or social accounts) and the location you choose (state, township). You can make your account private.
  • Private details: your birth date, gender and interests. We ask for your birth date to check your age (see "Children and age limits"). These are never shown on your profile.
  • What you post: posts, photos, comments, polls, shares, reactions and votes; communities you create or join.
  • Gămung Mart and Gămung Jobs: items you sell, jobs you post, and job applications you send, including any résumé (PDF) you attach.
  • Messages: your chats with other users, including photos and the posts, jobs or listings you share in them.
  • Support: messages you send to Gămung Support and the details that come with them (topic, app version, phone type).
  • Reports: what you report and why.

Information collected automatically

  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a device token so we can send them, and your app language.
  • Activity status: when you were last active, shown to people you message with. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy (this also hides others' status from you).
  • Basic records: when things were created or changed, and records our servers keep to run securely (for example, sign-in sessions).

We do not collect your contacts, your precise location, or your browsing outside the app.

How we use information

  • To run the app: show your profile and posts, deliver messages, run Mart, Jobs and communities.
  • To show what's relevant: for example, jobs, items and communities near the location you chose appear first, and listings from Gămung Verified accounts appear before others.
  • To keep Gămung safe: check ages for age-limited features, review reports, remove content that breaks our rules, stop scams and harassment, and suspend accounts when needed.
  • To support you: answer your messages and tell you about important changes to your account.
  • To send notifications you've allowed, such as new messages or job application updates.

What other people can see

  • Public by default: your name, photo, bio, profile links, location, followers count, posts in public communities, your Mart listings and jobs.
  • Private account: only approved followers see your profile posts. Your listings and jobs stay public so buyers and job seekers can find them.
  • Private communities: only members see posts inside them. Exception: when a post, comment or community in a private community is reported, the Gămung safety team can view what was reported (with its comments, for context) — only while the report is being handled and for a short time after — to investigate scams, harassment or other rule-breaking. The team does this without joining, cannot post there, and every such view is recorded.
  • Anonymous posts and comments: other users don't see who wrote them, but they are still linked to your account. The Gămung team can see who wrote them when looking into a report or a rule violation.
  • Job applications: the person who posted the job sees your application and résumé. Résumés are stored privately and opened only through short-lived links.
  • Messages: only you and the person you're chatting with see your messages in the app. Messages are stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted. We don't read your messages as a routine; authorised team members may look at them only to investigate a report, to protect someone's safety, or when the law requires it. Photos sent in messages are stored at hard-to-guess links; anyone who is given such a link can open it, so only send photos you're comfortable with.

Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to run Gămung:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, file storage and sign-in. Our servers are located in India (Mumbai).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Google and Apple: if you choose to sign in with them, they confirm your identity to us. We don't get your Google or Apple password.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail and Google Sheets): our team uses Gmail to reply by email and keeps a copy of support messages in a private Google Sheet to manage them.
  • Google Fonts: the app may download its fonts from Google's servers.
  • The law: we may share information if we're legally required to, or if it's necessary to protect someone from serious harm.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep information

  • While your account exists: we keep your information until you delete it or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account: your profile, posts, comments, listings, jobs, applications, messages and uploaded files are deleted right away. Communities you own alone are deleted too; communities with other members must be handed to someone else first.
  • What we keep after deletion: to keep the community safe and meet legal duties, we may keep: copies of content our team removed for breaking the rules (kept as evidence, with the reason); reports and the team's moderation records; and copies of your support messages and our replies (in our support Sheet and email). We keep these only as long as they're needed for safety or legal reasons.

Children and age limits

  • You must be at least 13 to use Gămung. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we'll delete it.
  • Selling in Gămung Mart, posting jobs and applying to jobs are for people 18 and over.
  • Your birth date can't be changed after you set it. If it's wrong, contact Gămung Support.

Your choices and rights

  • See and edit: you can view and change your profile and private details in the app.
  • Delete: delete posts, comments, listings and jobs at any time, or your whole account from Settings > Account & Security.
  • Privacy controls: make your account private, hide your activity status, block people, and mute notifications.
  • Get a copy or ask a question: write to gamung.app@gmail.com and we'll help.

If you live in the European Union or the UK, you also have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your information, to receive a copy in a portable form, and to complain to your data protection authority. We use your information to provide the service you asked for (contract), to keep Gămung safe and prevent fraud (legitimate interests), to meet legal duties, and with your consent where we ask for it (for example, notifications).

Security

We use encrypted connections, access rules that limit who can see what, and private storage for sensitive files such as résumés. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and don't share sign-in codes. Gămung will never ask for your password, sign-in codes or a payment in a message.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app.

Contact

Email: gamung.app@gmail.com

In the app: Settings > Help & Support

ဥရောပသမဂ္ဂ သို့မဟုတ် ယူကေတွင် နေထိုင်ပါက သင့်အချက်အလက်များကို ကြည့်ရှုခွင့်၊ ပြင်ဆင်ခွင့်၊ ဖျက်ခွင့်၊ ကန့်သတ်ခွင့်၊ ကန့်ကွက်ခွင့်၊ သယ်ယူနိုင်သော ပုံစံဖြင့် မိတ္တူရယူခွင့်နှင့် ဒေတာကာကွယ်ရေး အာဏာပိုင်ထံ တိုင်ကြားခွင့်တို့လည်း ရှိပါသည်။ သင်တောင်းဆိုသည့် ဝန်ဆောင်မှုပေးရန် (စာချုပ်)၊ Gămung ကို လုံခြုံစေပြီး လိမ်လည်မှုတားဆီးရန် (တရားဝင်အကျိုးစီးပွား)၊ ဥပဒေဆိုင်ရာ တာဝန်များအတွက်နှင့် ကျွန်ုပ်တို့ မေးမြန်းသည့်အခါ သင့်ခွင့်ပြုချက်ဖြင့် (ဥပမာ အသိပေးချက်များ) သင့်အချက်အလက်များကို အသုံးပြုပါသည်။

စာဝှက်ထားသော ချိတ်ဆက်မှုများ၊ မည်သူ ဘာမြင်ရမည်ကို ကန့်သတ်သည့် စည်းမျဉ်းများနှင့် ကိုယ်ရေးရာဇဝင်ကဲ့သို့ အရေးကြီးဖိုင်များအတွက် သီးသန့်သိုလှောင်မှုကို အသုံးပြုပါသည်။ မည်သည့်စနစ်မျှ အပြည့်အဝ လုံခြုံသည်မဟုတ်သဖြင့် ခိုင်မာသော စကားဝှက်ကိုသုံးပြီး ဝင်ရောက်ရန်ကုဒ်များကို မမျှဝေပါနှင့်။ Gămung သည် မက်ဆေ့ထဲတွင် သင့်စကားဝှက်၊ ဝင်ရောက်ရန်ကုဒ် သို့မဟုတ် ငွေပေးချေမှုကို ဘယ်တော့မှ မတောင်းပါ။

ဤမူဝါဒ ပြောင်းလဲမှုများ

အရေးကြီးသော ပြောင်းလဲမှုများ ပြုလုပ်ပါက ထိပ်ရှိ ရက်စွဲကို ပြင်ဆင်ပြီး အက်ပ်ထဲတွင် အသိပေးပါမည်။
